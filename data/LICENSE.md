# Data licence

The literature datasets in `data/literature/` and the tables in `data/` were compiled by
Savisha Mahalingam and are released under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
You may share and adapt them for any purpose, provided you cite the accompanying review
(see `CITATION.cff`).

The hourly weather files in `data/weather/` are unmodified NASA POWER downloads
(https://power.larc.nasa.gov). NASA POWER places no restrictions on their use and
requests that users cite the NASA POWER Project.
